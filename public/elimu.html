    Family follow-up

    Import families

    Columns
    • student student's name (required)
    • parent parent or guardian name
    • phone parent's phone number
    • centre which centre (optional). Fill it in and each centre manager can show just their own centre's list.
    • last_attended date of the student's last session (required). Use YYYY-MM-DD or DD/MM/YYYY (day first).
    • sessions_last_4_weeks sessions attended in the last 4 weeks (optional)
    • usual_sessions_per_4_weeks sessions they normally attend in 4 weeks (optional)
    • open_complaint what the parent complained about, if it is still open (leave blank if none)
    • complaint_date when the complaint was raised (optional, same date formats)

    A family is matched on student name plus centre, so re-importing updates them. If you also use the Termly fee tracker in this browser, any fee balance is shown next to the family.

    Term

    Starting a new term clears this term's outcomes and next actions so every flagged family is looked at again. Families, notes and history are kept. Every action is listed with its date and time under “Activity” at the bottom of the page, with a CSV download.

    Termly fee tracker

    For the fee office at Elimu Plus. Load the student list once, paste M-Pesa or bank payment messages as they come in, and see who has paid, who is part-paid and who is overdue at each centre, with a reminder ready to send to each parent. No login. Nothing leaves this browser.

    Paste payments

    One payment message per line, copied from M-Pesa, the bank or your own notes. Cortex reads the amount, the receipt code, the phone number, the account number and the payer's name, then matches each payment to a student by account number (student ID), parent phone or name. A receipt code is only ever counted once.
