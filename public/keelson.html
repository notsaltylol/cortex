OEM contracts: load a CRM export

Have last week's export? Paste it to compare now

Columns
  • account and/or opportunity (deal name): at least one is required
  • amount or value in $, e.g. 250000, $250,000 or 250k (required)
  • stage (required) and close_date as 2026-12-15 or 12/15/2026 (required)
  • owner, last_updated (optional; used for the check-in list)
  • probability as 60, 60% or 0.6 (optional; otherwise the stage table below is used)
  • opportunity_id (optional; makes week-over-week matching exact)

Each import is kept. The first import in a new week is compared with the last import from an earlier week, shown under "Changes since last Monday".
