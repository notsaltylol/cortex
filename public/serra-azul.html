Repeat-contact retention

Find postpaid customers who have contacted care more than once recently, decide who to call first, and check care-center capacity.

What this does and does not do

There is no CRM or contact-center integration. It works from a contact log you paste or upload. Everything stays in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

1. Repeat-contact list

Columns and rules
  • Needed columns: customer ID, contact date, plan type. Optional: channel, reason, center, monthly plan value. Common English and Portuguese header names are recognised.
  • A customer is flagged when the plan is postpaid and they have 2 or more contacts in the window. The window counts back from the latest date in the file, so with 30 days it covers the latest date and the 29 days before it. Prepaid and unlabelled plans are not flagged.
  • Order: more contacts first, then the same reason repeated (an unresolved issue), then a mention of cancelling or a competitor in the reason (cancelar, cancelamento, portabilidade, cancel, port out, switch, concorrente), then plan value.
