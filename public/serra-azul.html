ChurnRadar

Score every subscription account red/amber/green from usage, tickets and renewal date.

What this does and does not do

There is no CRM or contact-center integration. It works from an account export you paste or upload. Account rows are kept in this browser. The account fields above (usage change, open tickets or contacts, escalation, days to renewal, plan and share of lines active), not names or phone numbers, are sent to Cortex's scoring on this site, and only when "Also score with TypeSafe" is ticked. It is ticked by default; untick it for rules only.

1. Account health

Column mapping
  • Needed: an account or customer ID. Recognised if present, by common English and Portuguese header names: plan, plan type (postpaid or prepaid), center or region, monthly value, usage change over 30 days in % (or usage this month and usage last month, from which the % is computed), support contacts or tickets in the last 30 days, open tickets, escalated or complaint (yes/no, sim/nao), contract or loyalty end date (or days to renewal), and an optional reason text.
  • Dates as yyyy-mm-dd or dd/mm/yyyy. Numbers such as -22, -22% and 1.234,50 are read.
  • TypeSafe scoring receives: plan, usage change (0 if missing), contacts in 30 days or else open tickets (0 if missing), escalated (false if missing) and days to renewal (365 if missing). The share of lines active is sent if your export has it; otherwise activeSeats is sent as 100. Accounts are sent with a generated reference, not your account ID.
  • Accounts with too little data (fewer than two of contacts, usage change and renewal, and nothing alarming) are shown as Can't judge and are not sent to TypeSafe.
  • If the export has no plan-type column, all accounts are treated as in scope.
