Cortex for Brasswire Software

At-risk ticket triage

Finds tickets in the general queue that signal an account may cancel or not renew, shows how long they have waited, and hands each one to Customer Success with a factual note and a timestamped record.

No helpdesk integration: this works from a ticket export or pasted tickets. Nothing is read from or written back to your helpdesk.

Where data lives: tickets, verdicts and the handoff log are saved in this browser only. If the TypeSafe check is on, ticket text (up to 1,500 characters each) is sent to TypeSafe to be classified; untick it to run on rules alone.

What this moves: the time from a cancellation warning landing in the general queue to Customer Success seeing it. The tiles below measure that on your own export: hours in queue for flagged tickets, and how many have no CS handoff yet.

Columns
  • ticket_id ticket number (recommended; rows without one are numbered)
  • created_at when the ticket was created, e.g. 2026-11-09 08:14 or ISO 8601 (needed for hours in queue)
  • account customer company name (needed for the per-account roll-up)
  • arr annual recurring revenue in $ (optional), e.g. 48000 or 48k
  • plan or tier (optional)
  • subject and body or description (at least one is required)
  • status (optional), assignee or queue (optional; a queue or assignee naming Customer Success counts as already handed off)
