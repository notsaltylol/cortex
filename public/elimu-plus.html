About

Elimu Plus runs six learning centres across Nairobi offering after-school tuition, Saturday revision sessions and holiday bootcamps in maths, sciences and languages for secondary students preparing for KCSE and IGCSE exams.

Co-founded by two former teachers, the group charges termly fees and has grown mostly through word of mouth among parents.

On their radar

  • Popular Saturday revision sessions
  • Growing mainly through parent referrals
