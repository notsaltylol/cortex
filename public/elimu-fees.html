Termly fee tracker

For the fee office at Elimu Plus. Load the student list once, paste M-Pesa or bank payment messages as they come in, and see who has paid, who is part-paid and who is overdue at each centre, with a reminder ready to send to each parent. No login. Nothing leaves this browser.

Paste payments

One payment message per line, copied from M-Pesa, the bank or your own notes. Cortex reads the amount, the receipt code, the phone number, the account number and the payer's name, then matches each payment to a student by account number (student ID), parent phone or name. A receipt code is only ever counted once.
