    How the numbers are worked out

    • Projected weekly demand = last week's sales, moved by half of the week-on-week change (capped at 50%), plus any promo uplift you enter.
    • vs plan = last week's sales compared with the weekly figure the plan assumed. Past your threshold, the plan is flagged stale.
    • Stock runs out in = units on hand (stores + DC) divided by projected daily demand. A delivery that lands before that point extends it. One that lands after it leaves a gap, shown in the actions.
    • Reorder: if stock runs out sooner than the supplier lead time, a standard reorder can't land in time, and the row is red.
    • Stores low uses store sales (total less e-commerce) against store stock, and points to DC stock if there is any.

    This is a plain rules-based calculation on the numbers you enter. It is not a statistical forecast. Everything stays in this browser; nothing is uploaded.
